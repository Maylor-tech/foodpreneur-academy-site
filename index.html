Frequently Asked Questions

What is FoodPreneur Academy?

FoodPreneur Academy is an online learning platform specifically designed for independent restaurant owners. We provide expert training, resources, and tools to help you grow and manage your restaurant business more effectively.

Who are the courses designed for?

Our courses are designed for independent restaurant owners, managers, and aspiring restaurateurs who want to improve their business operations, increase profitability, and learn industry best practices.

How do I access the courses?

Once you sign up, you'll get immediate access to our course platform. You can learn at your own pace, access materials 24/7, and revisit lessons as needed. All you need is an internet connection and a device.

What kind of support do you offer?

We offer comprehensive support including email assistance, community forums, and regular Q&A sessions. Our team is available Monday through Friday to help you with any questions or challenges you face.

Can I get a refund if I'm not satisfied?

Yes, we offer a 30-day money-back guarantee. If you're not satisfied with our courses, simply contact our support team within 30 days of purchase for a full refund.

Do you offer any free resources?

Yes! We provide free templates, guides, and tools in our Resources section. These include menu costing sheets, staff scheduling templates, and more to help you get started.
